# وَصَل — Brand Identity Guide

## Brand Name
**وَصَل** (Wassal) — "to connect, to reach, to link"

## Meaning
- Arabic root و-ص-ل (w-s-l): to connect, arrive, reach
- Reflects: Moroccan connection + solidarity + reaching across regions
- Works as verb (وصل — he connected), noun (وصال — connection), adjective (موصول — connected)

## Tagline
"من طنجة إلى الكويرة، من حي إلى وطن"
(From Tangier to Lagouira, from neighborhood to nation)

## Colors
- Primary: #0F766E (Teal-700) — trust + calm
- Accent: #F59E0B (Amber-500) — badges + achievements
- Background: #FAFAFA (neutral light)
- Dark: #0A0A0A

## Typography
- Headings: Tajawal (Arabic display font)
- Body: IBM Plex Sans Arabic (readable body font)

## Logo Concept
- 8-pointed Moroccan star (zellige pattern) simplified to minimal lines
- Teal background circle + white star
- Clean, modern, scalable

## Hashtag
#wassal #وصل #وصال

## Voice
- Warm, inclusive, Moroccan
- Professional but accessible
- Arabic-first, multilingual
